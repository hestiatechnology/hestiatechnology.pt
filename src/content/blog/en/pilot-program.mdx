We are launching the Hestia Pilot Program for selected textile manufacturers, brands, and supply-chain teams.

The goal is simple: help your team start implementing Digital Product Passport processes in a practical way, with close support from our product and operations specialists.

## What you get in the pilot

- Guided onboarding and process mapping
- Support to structure product and material data
- Practical setup for traceability and DPP readiness
- Early access to upcoming platform features
- Direct feedback channel with the Hestia team

## Who should apply

The pilot is designed for organizations that are:

- Preparing for upcoming EU transparency and sustainability requirements
- Managing data across multiple suppliers or production stages
- Looking for a faster way to centralize compliance documentation
- Ready to collaborate actively and share implementation feedback

## Pilot timeline

We are currently accepting applications for the next pilot cohort. We plan to onboard around 10 companies in this phase while keeping applications open, and selected participants will be contacted to schedule an intro session and define scope.
